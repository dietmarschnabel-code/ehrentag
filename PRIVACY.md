# Privacy policy – Ehrentag-Manager / Special Days Manager

*Last updated: 15 November 2026*

Ehrentag-Manager (in English: Special Days Manager) is a small app for keeping track of birthdays, name days and other
dates worth remembering. It is free and open source ([source code](https://github.com/dietmarschnabel-code/ehrentag)).

## In short

**The app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.** There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and
no crash reporting. Everything you enter stays on your device.

## What the app stores

The names and dates you enter (for example "Maria Muster, birthday, 24 December 1980"), plus your settings (language,
display options, reminder options). They are stored in a file in the app's private storage on your device. Other apps
cannot read it. The app never sends this file anywhere.

## Permissions

- **Notifications (Android):** only if you switch on reminders. The app then asks for permission to show notifications and
  schedules them on your device. The notification text contains the name and type of the date you entered. Nothing leaves
  your device.

The app has no access to your contacts, calendar, location, camera, microphone or photos, and it does not connect to the
internet.

## Export, import and backups

Exporting a calendar file (.ics) or a backup (events.json) is something you start yourself. The file is created on your
device and goes only where you save or share it, for example to a folder, an e-mail or a calendar app. What happens to it
after that is governed by the app you give it to. Importing reads only the file you pick.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes all data it stored. Within the app you can delete single entries at any time.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.
